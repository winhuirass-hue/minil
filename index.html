Features

  • No glibc
  • No libstdc++
  • Direct Linux syscalls
  • malloc/free/realloc
  • mmap/munmap
  • snprintf
  • C++ new/delete

Philosophy

no hidden runtime, no implicit initialization, no abstraction over syscalls.
